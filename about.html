About — Social Parlor omnivore

A tiny 8-seat counter bar, one minute from Kachidoki Station.

"Omnivore" means an animal that eats everything — and the owner has happily devoured food, music, fashion, pro wrestling, martial arts and subculture. omnivore is a "social parlour": a small place to share stories, his and yours, over a drink.

  • No cover charge — one order per person
  • Drinks from ¥500 (tax included)
  • 130+ drinks: Japanese whisky, shochu, sake, craft gin and more
  • Japanese ramen and simple snacks
  • Solo visitors welcome — the owner speaks English
  • Full English menu, with descriptions for every item

Open from 2pm, so it's an easy stop after Tsukiji, Toyosu Market or Tsukishima monja street.

Good to know

Is there a cover charge?
No. Just order one item per person.
Do you speak English?
Yes, the owner speaks English, and the whole menu — names and descriptions — is available in English.
Can I come alone?
Of course — it's all counter seats, perfect for solo visitors.
How can I pay?
Cash, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), IC transit cards (Suica, PASMO) and QR payments.
Can I make a reservation?
Yes — send us a DM on Instagram (@omnivore_tokyo) or call +81 3-6204-9955. Please note that we sometimes miss Instagram DMs while we're busy behind the counter. If you don't get a reply, your reservation is not confirmed — please give us a call. Walk-ins are always welcome, too.
Is there Wi-Fi?
Yes, free Wi-Fi. The password is on the menu page.
How many seats?
8 counter seats.
